COSMOS2020 Classic / LePhare coverage diagnostics

Sample: the 34,792 galaxies previously plotted in results/cosmos2020_specz.
The DR1.1 unique spectroscopic catalog supplies one preferred measurement per
galaxy. Selection: public, flag in 3/4/13/14, photoz_type=0, valid Classic ID,
finite positive spec-z, finite nonnegative photo-z. No residual or photometric
quality cuts were added. Every galaxy matched exactly one valid PDF by ID.

PDF source:
https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/COSMOS/tables/cosmos2020/PZ/COSMOS2020_CLASSIC_R1_v2.1_LEPHARE_PZ_p3.fits.gz
Header: first row gives z grid; first column gives Classic object ID.
The completed range-assembled download is classic_lephare_complete.fits.gz.
The original interrupted single-stream download is explicitly marked .partial
and is not used. Extraction read the entire complete gzip stream and verified CRC.
Full matched PDFs are cached in data/cosmos2020_specz/classic_matched_pdfs.npz.

Reconstruction: nonnegative densities linearly interpolated between grid points,
individually normalized on z=0..10. Grid spacing is 0.01. CDFs are analytic
integrals of the linear segments. No broadening, prior correction, or recalibration.
PDF median agreement with archive medians is documented in coverage_summary.json.

TARP mass for each galaxy and reference r:
  f = P_PDF(|Z-r| <= |z_spec-r|).
The coverage curve is the empirical CDF P(f<=alpha). Calibration requires the
diagonal when validation truths follow the conditional population represented by
the PDFs. A diagonal plot alone is not proof of full conditional correctness.
Two choices of references are tested, with 64 independent draws per galaxy:
  global: Uniform(0,10)
  local: Uniform(max(0,m-.25*(1+m)), min(10,m+.25*(1+m)))
where m is the PDF median, which depends only on photometric information.
No reference distribution is chosen using the spectroscopic truth.
Integration replaces posterior Monte Carlo sampling but tests the same DRP regions.
Reference: Lemos et al. 2023, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/lemos23a.html

Equal-tailed intervals: alpha-coverage is abs(2*CDF(z_spec)-1)<=alpha.
PIT: CDF(z_spec). Extreme values are retained. Curves are anchored at (0,0)
and (1,1) for display; rounded zero-probability tails are reported separately.
Magnitudes are HSC_i_MAG_APER2 (2 arcsec aperture, no total correction).
Twenty galaxies have unavailable/invalid magnitudes and are retained in 'All'.

Shading: 200 galaxy-bootstrap resamples; pointwise 95% intervals. All reference
draws associated with a galaxy stay together. These bands omit cosmic variance,
spectroscopic selection, incorrect spec-z or matches, and shared systematics.

Numerical validation: calibrated synthetic PDFs pass; deliberately narrowed PDFs
fail; independent truth draws from the actual COSMOS PDFs recover the diagonal.
A piecewise-constant midpoint-bin reconstruction gives similar conclusions;
numerical_checks.json records the differences. The raw z-grid cannot resolve
sub-grid PDF shape, particularly for the narrowest bright-source distributions.

These diagnostics assess calibration on selected successful spectroscopy, not
the complete COSMOS photometric population. Undercoverage may reflect bias,
underestimated widths, incorrect mode/tail probabilities, selection, or wrong
reference redshifts. A single broadening factor is not established by this test.
