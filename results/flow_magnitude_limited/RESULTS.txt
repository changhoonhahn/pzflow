Revised magnitude-limited COSMOS posterior-transfer experiment

Parent: clean COSMOS2020 Classic galaxies, HSC i < 25 AB in 2 arcsec apertures,
native i S/N >= 5, usable six-band fluxes/errors, valid full LePhare PDFs.
The entire eligible catalog is retained, independently of spectroscopy.
170,003 input galaxies pass the magnitude and i-SNR cuts; 162,239 pass the
six-band measurements, a 95.43% retention fraction. This is not a measurement
of detection completeness. Galaxy classification and catalog detection remain
selection effects. No positive-flux or SNR cut in the other five bands.

Spatial splits: 111,952 train; 23,652 validation; 26,635 photometric test.
The scored subset is 5,083 secure public spec-z objects in the test cells.
All photometric test objects are excluded from both training and validation.

Model                         N_train   NMAD     Outliers  Cov68  Cov95
COSMOS PDFs, entire parent     111,952   0.01783  2.81%     68.6%  94.7%
COSMOS PDFs, matched objects    22,604   0.02202  3.13%     69.9%  94.3%
Spec-z baseline                22,604   0.02104  3.07%     66.0%  94.0%

All metrics use the same test galaxies and simulated noisy photometry.
NMAD uses (median predicted z - spec-z)/(1+spec-z); outliers have absolute
normalized residual > 0.15. Interval coverages use equal-tailed intervals.

The full COSMOS model has 15.3% lower scatter than the spec baseline. The
outlier difference is small and its paired-bootstrap 95% interval includes
zero. Its median normalized bias is -0.00636 versus +0.00014 for the baseline,
and its normalized RMSE is worse (0.1583 versus 0.1506).
At identical training objects/counts the PDF model has slightly worse scatter
and log density than the spec baseline. The apparent full-PDF advantage is
associated with the additional, differently selected photometric population;
this experiment does not separate the effects of its size and selection.

At z>2 only 167 test objects remain. Full-PDF coverage68 is 63.5%, versus
70.7% for the spec baseline; high-z calibration is not established.

Evaluation selection remains strong: native median i is 24.17 for the full
photometric test population and 22.69 for its spec subset. Only 2.35% of
selected objects at 24.5 <= i < 25 have qualifying secure public spectroscopy.
No claim of population-wide calibration is warranted from this test.

This remains a CFHT-u/HSC-grizy native-filter experiment with LSST-like noise,
not an actual LSST bandpass or measured-Rubin experiment. COSMOS PDFs are
treated as unbiased; no PDF recalibration or prior division was applied.
Single training seed; galaxy bootstrap does not include training variance or
spatial covariance. Independence from the teacher's original spectroscopic
template/zero-point calibration has not been audited.

Historical oversampled results remain in results/flow_experiment; their test
population differs and their numbers should not be compared directly here.
